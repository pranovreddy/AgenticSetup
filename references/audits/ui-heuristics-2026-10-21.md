# UI heuristics audit: DigiConsent agentic onboarding (PM feedback file)

**File:** `agentic-setup-cards-states-pm-feedback.html` (artifact v26) · **Date:** 21 Oct 2026 · **Screenshots:** `ui-heuristics-2026-10-21/`

**Method.** Playwright and Chromium, Google Fonts blocked. 1440×900 at 1x and 2x, spot checks at 1280×720 and 1920×1080, and `reducedMotion: reduce`. I walked the real flow end to end: scrolled and accepted both agreements, Start AI setup, processing, ready, submitted the profile, sent the Team invites, opened View and edit then confirmed Brands, GTM and Templates, then the finale. All 48 Prototype menu states were forced with `__deck.uxState()`. Evidence comes from screenshots, a DOM pass (effective font size after transforms and zoom, WCAG contrast against the composited background, target size), Tab-order walks, and `document.getAnimations()`.
**Not reported (known):** sample Templates figures, soft text in the 3D stacks, and HANDOFF "Open items". **Not reached:** "Do it yourself" filling (an open item) and true network loss (only the forced offline state).

## Fix these five first
1. **C1 and C2**: make the KYC fields editable, and name the operators on the submit button and above the tick. This is the highest-stakes, irreversible action.
2. **M1**: `inert` on the gate, hidden stepper and non-focus cards. One change fixes keyboard use on every screen.
3. **M2**: restore the 17px floor. Cap the card scale and remove the 85% zoom on edit pages.
4. **M4 and M7**: tell the truth at the moments things leave the bank: sequence "sending" and "sent", and say on Confirm mapping that templates are sent for approval.
5. **M3 and M9**: use one deadline source on the finale, and give the closed window a non-success treatment.

## Working well, keep it
- **Agreement gate:** scroll-to-accept with a live "% read", "accepting on behalf of HDFC Bank Limited as Ravi Menon", a plain-language summary marked "only a guide", and clear load-fail and refusal recovery (`gate-fail`, `gate-refused`).
- **Locking during agent work:** the KYC tick and Submit stay disabled until filling finishes (verified every 1.5 s).
- **Error and edge states:** they name the problem and offer Try again, Do it yourself or Do it later, e.g. "Two tries didn't work", "Your changes so far are kept", "The rest of your setup is fine". The concurrent-edit dialog names who submitted and when.
- **Reduced motion** is honoured. With `reduce`, the ring stops and 0 infinite animations run.
- **Visible focus ring** on the primary buttons (2px dark outline). Status chip colours are consistent (Needs attention orange, In review amber, Do it later violet, Done green).
- **Brands edit page:** the "Proof: seen in DLT header" chip and the Consent Wallet phone preview give strong evidence and a strong mental model. The GTM edit page's BEST cells make the comparison easy to scan.
