Layout directions

Four ways to give the content the full space, keep the card-transition idea, and stop the credit-card look. Each shows the agent processing and the review step at 1440 x 900. Click a picture to enlarge.
AWorkspace + Rail
One big workspace, and the other steps live in a slim stepper on the left as mini cards. Only the current step gets the full width.
Short windows: the rail collapses to icon-only tiles and the sheet scrolls inside with its action bar pinned, so text never shrinks. Processing: the stage plays in the sheet, not in a card. Unlock: the next tile fills in and slides the workspace over.

BTop Dock
Five mini cards sit in a dock across the top (progress under each). The current step opens as a full-width sheet below. Finished steps tick green, upcoming ones show what unlocks them.
Short windows: the dock shrinks to a 56px strip of icons and progress, the sheet takes the rest, and its footer stays pinned. Unlock: the dock tile expands and the sheet changes with a slide.

CAgent Console
The agent has a dedicated blue panel on the left (what it did, what it needs from you). The work surface on the right has step pills on top and uses the full width for content.
Short windows: the console collapses to a slim bar with the agent line and the 'Needs you' note; the surface gets the full height. Processing is a live timeline plus the form filling in.

DMission Board + Folder Tabs
No cards at all. Processing is a board of five lanes with live progress, the active lane expanding to show the agent at work. Review uses folder tabs: the open step is a tall sheet, the others are locked tabs that unlock one by one.
Short windows: lanes collapse to single rows and the active one scrolls; in review the tabs shrink to icons and the sheet scrolls with a pinned footer. Three-column forms keep the fields large and short.
